    Decompile any EVM contract. Then read what it really does.

    evmdec turns deployed bytecode, fetched by address on any EVM chain, or freshly compiled bytecode into structured pseudo-Solidity: functions with selectors, parameters and mutability, storage layout, events, internal helpers, revert reasons. It then analyses the result for reentrancy, unchecked calls, delegatecall and selfdestruct exposure, missing access control, proxies, weak randomness and unchecked arithmetic, and reports each finding with the lines it is about.

    Paste the creation or runtime bytecode of a contract you are about to deploy to review it the way an attacker would see it, or an address to inspect what is live. Proxies are followed to their implementation. Every basic block's lifted semantics are checked against a concrete EVM interpreter on random inputs; the measured numbers are in the README.
